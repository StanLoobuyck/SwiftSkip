Settings · version

General

Turn off to use the player without SwiftSkip's shortcuts and extras.

Skip interval

How far the skip shortcuts jump.

Speed steps

How much faster or slower each press makes it (0.25× – 4×).

New lectures start at the speed you last chose.

Reopening a lecture jumps back to where you stopped.

Keyboard shortcuts

Click a key to change it, or + to add a second one. While recording, Esc cancels and Backspace removes the key. Press ? on a lecture to see them all.

About

SwiftSkip is an independent, open-source student project. It is not affiliated with, endorsed by or supported by KU Leuven. It collects no data.
